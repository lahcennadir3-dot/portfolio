03 — How I Work

Clear steps. No surprises.

The rules every project runs on, agreed before any design starts.

01

Brief & Direction

Goals, audience, references, and visual direction — agreed before any design starts.

02

Payment

50% to start, 50% on delivery. Work begins once the deposit is received.

03

Revisions

2 rounds of revision included per project. Further rounds are quoted separately.

04

Timeline

Turnaround depends on project size — agreed and confirmed before we start.

05

Final Delivery

Exported files, ready for web, print, or social — organized and labeled.

06

After Delivery

Once the final files are approved and sent, the project closes. New changes are a new project.
