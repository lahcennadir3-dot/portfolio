Brand Identity

Boltex Nutrition

Brand Identity / Sports Nutrition

  • Logo Design
  • Packaging
  • Bottle Design
  • Brand Mockups
  • Digital Assets
01

Challenge

Most sports nutrition brands lean on loud colors and generic athletic clichés. Boltex needed to feel serious and performance-driven without shouting for attention on a crowded shelf.

02

Creative Direction

A stripped-back black-and-white palette with a single red accent, built around bold, condensed typography — a visual language borrowed from performance gear rather than typical supplement packaging.

03

Logo / Identity

The wordmark and icon system built for Boltex — bold, geometric, and built to read clearly at any size, from a bottle cap to a billboard.
